A record of communication

Email tells a story.

Years of projects, decisions, relationships, and everyday exchanges accumulate across mailboxes and machines. Email Collection Toolkit brings those records into one durable collection.

Built for trust

Preservation without lock-in.

The archive remains useful without the application. Search indexes and reports are derivatives; the preserved mail and integrity information stand on their own.

  • BagIt 1.0 / Mailbag 1.0 archive
  • Original message data in MBOX
  • SHA-256 integrity verification
  • Source provenance for each message
  • MBOX interoperability with ePADD
