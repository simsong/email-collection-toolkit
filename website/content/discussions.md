Visit the [Community Forum](https://github.com/simsong/email-collection-toolkit/discussions)
to ask questions and discuss email collection workflows.

Announcements about the project will be posted in the
[Email Collection Toolkit Announcements discussion](https://github.com/simsong/email-collection-toolkit/discussions/55).

Please post proposed capabilities and workflow changes in the
[Feature Requests discussion](https://github.com/simsong/email-collection-toolkit/discussions/56).
For bug reports, [Contact Support](https://github.com/simsong/email-collection-toolkit/issues)
through the project's issue tracker after checking existing reports. Inspect
diagnostic reports before sharing them; they may contain private email text.
